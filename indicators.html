Indicators

A set of indicators to plan and to monitor research and innovation on sustainable plastics

Provisional results. The corpora are being validated: every number may change.

The indicators fall into three families. Together they answer three questions about any actor (a country, a university, a company): how much it does and on what, how connected it is, and how much its research reaches industry.

Family Indicator What it measures
Size and specialisation Documents Publications or patent families, 1980–2025 and 2015–2025
World share The actor’s share of the world’s documents in 2015–2025, per mille (‰)
Momentum World share in 2015–2025 divided by world share in 2000–2014: above 1, the actor grows faster than the world
Most specialised category The category C1–C8 where the actor’s share is highest compared with the world’s (at least 5 documents)
Specialisation index The actor’s share in a category divided by the world’s share: above 1, more than the world average
Network position Partners How many different organisations (or countries) it published or applied with
Joint documents How many documents it shares with those partners
Bridging position Betweenness centrality as a percentile (100 = highest): how often the actor lies on the shortest path between two others, i.e. how much it connects groups that would otherwise be apart
Links with industry Publications cited by patents Publications cited in patents (data: Reliance on Science, Marx and Fuegi)
Owners of the citing patents Who uses the research: companies, Italian applicants
Joint work with companies Publications written with companies; patents filed with companies
Patents only Granted Share of the families with at least one granted patent
Citations Mean number of later patents citing the family

How to keep the indicators up to date

The indicators are computed by scripts, so they can be renewed every year: the two collections of documents are downloaded again with the same keywords (OpenAlex for publications, PATSTAT for patents), the analysis scripts are run again, and this website is rebuilt. Comparing one year’s numbers with the previous year’s shows whether an actor is growing, changing its profile, or building new links.
